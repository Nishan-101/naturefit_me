Privacy note

Nature Fit Restaurant, Al Safa Complex, Dubai, stores the name, email, UAE mobile number, delivery address, and dietary notes you submit so the kitchen can prepare and deliver a meal plan.

Card numbers are not stored on this website. Card payments, when enabled, are handled by the payment provider's secure page. Bank transfer details you receive are for paying Nature Fit. Payment status, amount, currency, method, and transaction reference are stored with the order.

WhatsApp messages are sent only if you opt in separately. Click-to-chat opens a draft on your phone. It is not sent until you press send. Automatic WhatsApp messages are used only when Nature Fit has connected an approved WhatsApp Business template.

Staff can see orders in the dashboard. They are not published. To ask about your details, email naturefitdxb@gmail.com or call +971 50 248 3083.
